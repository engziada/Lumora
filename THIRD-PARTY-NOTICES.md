## App icons and brand logos

The launcher never redistributes application artwork. It displays the icon installed on the
device, and the optional "HD logos" mode fetches a publisher favicon at runtime from Google's
favicon service. All trademarks remain the property of their owners.

## Wallpapers

Online wallpaper mode links to photographs hosted by Unsplash and downloads them for personal
display on the device; they are not redistributed with this repository. The offline scenes are
generated procedurally at runtime.
