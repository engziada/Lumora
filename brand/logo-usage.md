# Logo usage

## Elements

| Asset | File | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mark (dark surfaces) | `assets/logo/lumora-mark-dark.svg`, `@drawable/ic_lumora_mark` | Bronze-to-amber blade and wave, night arch with crescent, stars and dunes, three diamonds |
| Mark (light surfaces) | `assets/logo/lumora-mark-light.svg` | Espresso blade, wave and diamonds |
| Wordmark (dark surfaces) | `assets/logo/lumora-wordmark-light.svg`, `@drawable/ic_lumora_wordmark` | Cream strokes, amber star in the O |
| Wordmark (light surfaces) | `assets/logo/lumora-wordmark-dark.svg` | Espresso strokes, copper star |
| App icon | `@drawable/ic_lumora_icon` | Mark on a rounded espresso tile with an amber glow |
| TV banner | `@drawable/banner_lumora` | 320 x 180 dp; mark left, wordmark right |

The mark reads as an **L**: the blade is the stem, the wave is the foot, and the arched window opens onto a moonlit desert.

## Clear space

Keep a margin of at least the width of the largest diamond (about 8% of the mark width) on every side. Between the mark and the wordmark, keep at least the height of the O.

## Minimum size

- Mark: 24dp. Below 32dp the stars may disappear; that is acceptable.
- Wordmark: 10dp cap height.

## Do

- Place the dark-surface mark on espresso (`#140A06` to `#3A2216`) or on dark, warm photography.
- Use the light-surface mark on cream or paper (`#E9DCCB` or lighter).
- Scale the mark proportionally.

## Don't

- Recolour the mark in cool colours (blue, violet, teal).
- Remove the arch, moon or wave, or rearrange the diamonds.
- Add drop shadows, outer glows or bevels; the glow is already inside the arch.
- Typeset "Lumora" in a font in place of the wordmark.
- Stretch, rotate or place the mark on busy, high-contrast backgrounds.
