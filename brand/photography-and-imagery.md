# Photography and imagery

## Wallpapers

- **Offline scenes** (default): procedurally rendered warm gradients with an amber or moonlight glow. They need no network.
- **Online stills** (opt-in): landscape photographs from Unsplash, requested at 1280 x 720 and cached.
- Wallpapers sit under the warm scrim (`bg_scrim`) so cream text always keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast.
- Preferred subjects: night skies, deserts, dunes, warm-lit architecture, still water at dusk. Avoid cold neon and busy, high-contrast scenes.

## Posters

TV posters come from TVmaze in 2:3 format. Show them unaltered on a 12dp-radius frame, with the title and channel below in `nova_text` and `nova_text_dim`.
