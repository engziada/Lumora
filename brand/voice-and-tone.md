# Voice and tone

Lumora speaks like a calm host at the start of an evening: warm, brief and confident.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Brief | "Tonight on TV" | "Here are all the shows that are scheduled to air today" |
| Warm | "Welcome back. 4 pinned, 2 hidden." | "User session restored." |
| Honest | "Needs one-time permission" | "Optimizing your experience…" |
| Plain | "Clear memory" | "Turbo boost" |

## Rules

- Address the viewer as "you"; refer to the app as "Lumora".
- Use sentence case for buttons and settings; all caps only for eyebrows and greetings.
- Errors say what happened and what to do next: "Listings unavailable. Check the connection and try again."
- No exclamation marks, no emoji in the UI.
- Never claim an action succeeded unless the system confirmed it.
